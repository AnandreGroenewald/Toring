# Stapel 1.21 — Saam gly (release notes)

1.21.2 (12102): Saam gly holds on phones (back after WhatsApp), side by side, find an opponent (the server's lobby),
the River run opens without a freeze. Notes:

af (363): Stapel 1.21: Saam gly! Tik 👥 en kies: nooi ’n vriend met ’n skakel, of soek enigiemand wat nou ook gly. Julle gly langs mekaar op dieselfde rivier en sien mekaar; wie die langste op die plank bly, wen. Jou rit hou nou ook as jy na WhatsApp toe gaan en terugkom. Ook: dryfpype op oop water, ’n kopliggie in die nag, ’n regte roompastei, en Afgly maak vinniger oop.

en (380): Stapel 1.21: Ride with a friend! Tap 👥 and choose: invite a friend with a link, or find anyone riding right now. You ride side by side on the same river and see each other; whoever stays on the board longest wins. Your ride now holds when you pop over to WhatsApp and back. Also: floating pipes on open water, a headlamp at night, a real cream pie, and the River run opens faster.

1.21.1 (12101): the lanes' marks are now floating pipes on the outer edges, only on open water (the owner: "Its just
for the outter lines ... just if you exit a canyon"). Notes for 1.21.1:

af (330): Stapel 1.21: Saam gly! Tik 👥 Saam gly en stuur die skakel vir ’n vriend: julle gly dieselfde rivier op dieselfde tyd en sien mekaar op die water. Wie kom die verste? Ook nuut: dryfpype wys die rivier se kante op oop water, ’n kopliggie in die nag, Hanswors se pastei is nou ’n regte roompastei, en nuwe spelers kies eers hul naam.

en (331): Stapel 1.21: Ride with a friend! Tap 👥 and send the link to a friend: you ride the same river at the same time and see each other on the water. Who gets the furthest? Also new: floating pipes show the river’s edges on open water, a headlamp at night, Hanswors’s pie is now a real cream pie, and new players choose their name first.

## Data safety
Unchanged. Saam gly passes your nickname and where you are on the river to the friend you invited, through the same
live rooms Block Clash uses; nothing is stored (the privacy pages say so, section "Saam gly"). The first-name sheet uses
the nickname the game already had.
