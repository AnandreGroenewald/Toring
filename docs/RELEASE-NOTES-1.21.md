# Stapel 1.21.0 — Saam gly (release notes)

Play Console release notes (internal testing), each under 500 characters.

## af (313 characters)
Stapel 1.21: Saam gly! Tik 👥 Saam gly en stuur die skakel vir ’n vriend: julle gly dieselfde rivier op dieselfde tyd en sien mekaar op die water. Wie kom die verste? Ook nuut: wit strome wys die drie bane, ’n kopliggie in die nag, Hanswors se pastei is nou ’n regte roompastei, en nuwe spelers kies eers hul naam.

## en-AU, en-CA, en-GB, en-US, en-ZA (318 characters)
Stapel 1.21: Ride with a friend! Tap 👥 and send the link to a friend: you ride the same river at the same time and see each other on the water. Who gets the furthest? Also new: white-water lines show the three lanes, a headlamp at night, Hanswors’s pie is now a real cream pie, and new players choose their name first.

## Data safety
Unchanged. Saam gly passes your nickname and where you are on the river to the friend you invited, through the same
live rooms Block Clash uses; nothing is stored (the privacy pages say so, section "Saam gly"). The first-name sheet uses
the nickname the game already had.
