## Google Play Data safety for 1.20 (draft; check against the form before sending)

Already declared (12 Oct 2026, 1.14): Name (nickname), Email (ephemeral), User IDs, Device or other IDs, App
interactions, Other actions. What Ons dorp adds, and where it goes on the form:

- **Device or other IDs**: also the random Ons dorp player number (made on the phone, with a secret code kept only as
  a fingerprint on the server). Already declared: add "and Ons dorp" to your notes; same answers (collected, not
  shared, required, App functionality + Account management).
- **Personal info > Name**: the nickname now also shows to crewmates and the other crews on Saturday's river. Same
  answers (collected, not shared, optional).
- **App activity > Other user-generated content**: NEW — the crew's name (chosen by its founder, checked for rude
  words) and the Weekly Tower's floors each player builds. Collected, not shared, required for Ons dorp, App
  functionality.
- **App activity > App interactions**: already declared; Ons dorp's Daily heights, rides, bombs and the latest Daily
  tower's blocks are game activity.
- Data deletion: Saturday rivers delete themselves after 6 weeks; crews and player records on request
  (https://stapelspel.pages.dev/privaatheid#p9).
- "Shared" stays No: other players seeing your nickname and tower inside the game is not sharing with a third party.
