NAME (max 30)
Stapel: Afrikaans tower game

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Stack blocks as high as you can. A new tower every day, the same for everyone!

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Stapel is a free, colourful tower-stacking game from South Africa, in Afrikaans or English.

The crane swings the block back and forth, faster the higher you build. Tap at the right moment, drop it and build your tower as high as you dare. Land a block exactly on top of the last one for a Perfect, build a combo and score extra points.

🏗️ DAILY TOWER
One new tower every day: the same blocks and the same weather for everyone. See where you stand on the day's leaderboard, how many players you beat, and share your result on WhatsApp. Want another go? Buy "Nog 'n kans" (one more chance) with coins, up to four times a day – the leaderboard keeps your best tower.

⛅ WEATHER YOU CAN FEEL
Wind pushes your blocks, rain makes everything slippery, a heat wave speeds up the crane, hail hits your tower and fog hides the landing. After the rain a rainbow may bring double points.

🐒 VISITORS
Blouaap the monkey waits for your next block: land it Perfect, or he throws blocks into the sea. Skelm Sakkie tries to steal blocks – tap him quickly to catch him. And five Perfects in a row bring Hanswors the clown with gifts: a big log that sets your tower solid.

☁️ HIGHER AND HIGHER
At 50 metres you're above the clouds, at 100 metres the aeroplanes fly by, at 200 metres you see the curve of the Earth – and then space and the stars. The water rises while you build: stay above the flood line!

⚔️ CHALLENGER SERIES
Play another player, a friend or Robot Rikus, in two ways:
• Race: you build at the same time with the same blocks. Whoever reaches 10, 20, 30 and 40 metres first chooses a punishment for the other tower (the monkey, the thief, fog or a heat wave). First to 50 metres wins!
• Block by Block: one tower, a block each in turn, three hearts each. Later come rounds with the same weather or visitor for both of you, and five Perfects in a row earn you a sabotage for the other player.
Challenge a friend with a link on WhatsApp (busy? one tap says "Sorry, busy right now"), pause together, play the same friend again and tease each other with an emoji 😂😛. If your connection drops, the game reconnects by itself. Climb the ranks every month from Bronze to Stapel Legend 👑, in each mode separately.

🪙 COINS, THE WEEKLY CHEST AND THE SHOP
Earn coins with every tower and every match, and open your weekly chest every day. In the Shop you buy power-ups for Practice – a Foundation block that sets everything under it like cement, a slow crane, a shield against the monkey and the thief, and an extra heart – and your own frame, badge, title and win celebration. In the Daily Tower everyone gets a free Foundation block at 55 metres.

🧱 PRACTICE
Practise as much as you like. It doesn't count.
