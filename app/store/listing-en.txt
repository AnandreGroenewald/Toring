NAME (max 30)
Stapel: Afrikaans tower game

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Stack blocks as high as you can. A new tower every day, the same for everyone!

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Stapel is a free, colourful tower-stacking game, made in Afrikaans.

The crane swings the block back and forth. Tap at the right moment, drop it and build your tower as high as you dare. Land a block exactly on top of the last one for a Perfek, build a combo and score extra points.

🏗️ DAILY TOWER
One new tower every day: the same blocks and the same weather for everyone, one try a day. See how many players you beat today, share your result on WhatsApp and see who builds highest.

⛅ WEATHER YOU CAN FEEL
Wind pushes your blocks, rain makes everything slippery, a heat wave makes the crane race, hail hits your tower and fog hides the landing. After the rain a rainbow may bring double points.

🐒 VISITORS
Blouaap the monkey comes to shove, Skelm Sakkie tries to steal blocks and Hanswors the clown brings a gift. Tap quickly to chase them off!

🌊 THE FLOOD
The water rises while you build. Stay above the flood line!

⚔️ CHALLENGER SERIES
Head-to-head on exactly the same tower. Whoever reaches 10, 20, 30 and 40 metres first sends a visitor to the other tower, and the first to 50 metres wins. Find an opponent and play live, challenge a friend with a link, or play Robot Rikus.

🧱 PRACTICE
Practise as much as you like. It doesn't count.

• Free
• No sign-up
• Works offline (only live matches need internet)
• Your progress stays on your phone
