NAME (max 30)
Stapel: Afrikaans tower game

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Stack blocks as high as you can. A new tower every day, the same for everyone!

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Stapel is a free, colourful tower-stacking game from South Africa, in Afrikaans or English.

The crane swings the block back and forth, faster the higher you build. Tap at the right moment, drop it and build your tower as high as you dare. Land a block exactly on top of the last one for a Perfect, build a combo and score extra points.

🏗️ DAILY TOWER
One new tower every day: the same blocks and the same weather for everyone, one try a day. See how many players you beat today, share your result on WhatsApp and see who builds highest.

⛅ WEATHER YOU CAN FEEL
Wind pushes your blocks, rain makes everything slippery, a heat wave makes the crane race, hail hits your tower and fog hides the landing. After the rain a rainbow may bring double points.

🐒 VISITORS
Blouaap the monkey waits for your next block: land it Perfect, or he throws blocks into the sea. Skelm Sakkie tries to steal blocks – tap him quickly to catch him. And Hanswors the clown builds you a new foundation.

🌊 THE FLOOD
The water rises while you build. Stay above the flood line!

⚔️ CHALLENGER SERIES
Head-to-head on exactly the same tower. Whoever reaches 10, 20, 30 and 40 metres first chooses a punishment for the other tower (the monkey, the thief, fog or a heat wave), and the first to 50 metres wins. Find an opponent and play live, challenge a friend with a link, or play Robot Rikus. Climb from Bronze to Diamond in monthly seasons, and show off your own frame, badge, title and win celebration.

🪙 COINS AND POWER-UPS
Earn coins with every tower and buy power-ups for Practice in the Shop: a Foundation block that sets everything under it like cement, a slow crane, a shield against the monkey and the thief, and an extra heart. In the Daily Tower everyone gets a free Foundation block at 55 metres.

🧱 PRACTICE
Practise as much as you like. It doesn't count.
