FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Stapel is a free, colourful tower-stacking game from South Africa, in Afrikaans or English. Build today's tower, then dive off the top and ride the river as far as you can.

🏗️ THE DAILY TOWER
One new tower every day: the same blocks and the same weather for everyone. The crane swings faster the higher you build: tap at the right moment, land a block exactly on the last one for a Perfect and build a combo. You get 3 free tries a day, and your best one counts on the day's leaderboard. Share your result on WhatsApp and see how many players you beat.

🌊 THE RIVER RUN
When your tower is done, your builder dives off and rides the river on a board. Swipe between three lanes, jump and duck past crocodiles, hippos, logs and whirlpools, collect coins and see how far you get: the further you go, the wilder the river (elephants at 10 km!). Ride whenever you like and climb the day's River run ranking.

👥 DUO RIDE
Ride the same river live, side by side with a friend: send an invite on WhatsApp, or find an opponent right away. Whoever stays on the board the longest wins.

⛅ WEATHER AND VISITORS
Wind pushes your blocks, rain makes everything slippery, a heat wave speeds up the crane and fog hides the landing. Blouaap the monkey waits for your next block, Skelm Sakkie tries to steal blocks and Hanswors the clown brings gifts. And the water rises while you build: stay above the flood line!

⚔️ CHALLENGER SERIES
Play another player, a friend or Robot Rikus head-to-head: a race on the same tower, or Block by Block, one block each in turn. Climb the ranks every month, from Bronze to Stapel Legend.

🪙 COINS AND THE SHOP
Earn coins with every tower, every ride and every match, and open your weekly chest every day. Coins come from playing only; they can't be bought. In the Shop you choose your tower's style (backgrounds, cranes and block skins), power-ups and your own frame, badge, title and win celebration.

🧱 PRACTICE
Practise as much as you like. It doesn't count.
